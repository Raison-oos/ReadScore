About READScore

Automated reading comprehension scoring for Grade 6 — Phil-IRI aligned, built to give teachers their time back.

Why we built it

Grading open-ended reading comprehension answers by hand is slow, and it's hard to stay perfectly consistent across dozens of students and questions. READScore reads each student's answer the way a careful grader would — checking whether the meaning matches the answer key, not just the wording — and does it in seconds instead of hours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Build a class

    A teacher creates a class, gets a share code, and students join with it — no separate rosters to manage.

  2. 2

    Add a passage and questions

    Write a reading passage and comprehension questions with an answer key. READScore automatically classifies each question by Bloom's Taxonomy level.

  3. 3

    Students read and respond

    Students read the passage and answer in their own words — no multiple choice required.

  4. 4

    Instant, weighted scoring

    Each answer is scored for semantic similarity to the answer key, weighted by the question's Bloom's level, and mapped to a Phil-IRI reading level.

AUTO-CLASSIFICATION
Bloom's Level Detection

A fine-tuned BERT classifier tags each question's cognitive level automatically — no manual labeling.

BERTSCORE
Contextual Similarity

Measures semantic overlap between student and reference answers using BERT embeddings.

BLOOM'S WEIGHT
Cognitive-Level Scoring

Questions are weighted by cognitive level — from Remember to Analyze — for richer insights.

PHIL-IRI
Official Reading Levels

Results map directly to Independent, Instructional, or Frustration reading levels.
