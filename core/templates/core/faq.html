Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how READScore works.

READScore is a reading comprehension assessment tool for Grade 6, aligned with the Phil-IRI framework. Teachers create classes and assign reading passages with open-ended questions; student answers are scored automatically for meaning, not exact wording.

Each answer is compared to the teacher's answer key for semantic similarity (using BERTScore) — a paraphrased but correct answer still scores well. That match percentage is then weighted by the question's Bloom's Taxonomy level before being combined into your final score.

These are the three Phil-IRI reading levels your final score maps to: Independent (80% and above) means you can comprehend the passage on your own, Instructional (59–79%) means you'd benefit from guided support, and Frustration (below 59%) means the passage is currently too difficult without help.

Every question is automatically classified by Bloom's Taxonomy level. Higher-order questions — Analyze, Evaluate, Create — are weighted more heavily than simpler Remember/Understand questions, since they demonstrate deeper comprehension.

Ask your teacher for the class code, then use the "Join Class" option on your classroom page to enter it. Once joined, every test your teacher assigns to that class will show up there.

Submitting a test again replaces your previous answers and score for that test, so check with your teacher before resubmitting — some classes may expect a single attempt.

AUTO-CLASSIFICATION
Bloom's Level Detection

A fine-tuned BERT classifier tags each question's cognitive level automatically — no manual labeling.

BERTSCORE
Contextual Similarity

Measures semantic overlap between student and reference answers using BERT embeddings.

BLOOM'S WEIGHT
Cognitive-Level Scoring

Questions are weighted by cognitive level — from Remember to Analyze — for richer insights.

PHIL-IRI
Official Reading Levels

Results map directly to Independent, Instructional, or Frustration reading levels.
